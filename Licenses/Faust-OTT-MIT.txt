The port uses the MIT function's fitted dynamics constants, curves, and
detector and release laws. BREACH uses its existing C++ crossover and
control-rate infrastructure. Adjustment range, silence protection, makeup
and boost limits are tuned for BREACH's pre-resonator signal chain.
